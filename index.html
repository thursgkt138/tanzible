Good to know

Questions,
answered.

What is Tanzible?

Tanzible is a planned platform for discovering products at participating physical stores and reserving them for in-store pickup.

Does Tanzible deliver products?

No. The initial experience is designed around pickup from physical retailers.

Where will Tanzible launch?

Bengaluru is the planned launch city. The service is not yet live.

Can retailers join?

Retailers can register their interest in participating. Retailer onboarding and catalogue tools are planned for later development.

Can I reserve products right now?

Not yet. Product cards are a demonstration, and live inventory and reservations are not connected.
